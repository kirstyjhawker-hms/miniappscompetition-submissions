## Description

Play 20+ classic, brain, action, and puzzle games online or locally, compete with friends, and earn NIM rewards as you play.

## Builder story

I've always been passionate about video games, especially arcade and casual games that you can pick up and enjoy anytime on your phone. Growing up, I spent countless hours playing simple but addictive games, and they inspired me to start building games of my own.

This project is my way of combining that passion with the Nimiq ecosystem. I want to create a collection of fun, polished, and highly replayable mini-games that people genuinely enjoy, while also bringing more users and engagement to the Nimiq community. My goal is to make something that players come back to because it's entertaining, not just because it's part of a Web3 application.

I believe games are one of the best ways to introduce people to new technologies in a natural and enjoyable way. If someone discovers Nimiq because they had fun playing these games, then the project has already achieved something meaningful. Building experiences that people enjoy while helping grow the community is what motivates me the most.
